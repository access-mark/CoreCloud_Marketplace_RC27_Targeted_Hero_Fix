PLATFORM OVERVIEW

Software governance, not business-data collection.

CoreLogiX is CoreCloud’s sovereign software control-plane initiative. Its architectural role is to manage installation identity, entitlements, licensing, health, lifecycle and controlled release processes—not to collect customer business data.

Identity and registration

Establish and manage the identity of installed products and their governing authority.

Licensing and entitlement

Support activation and policy-based entitlement and lease decisions.

Health and lifecycle

Receive product heartbeat and health signals and support lifecycle governance.

Release control

Provide a governed foundation for product versioning, distribution and release oversight.

CoreLogiX has documented development and test milestones. Production availability, certification scope and customer deployment are not asserted here.
ARCHITECTURE

A control plane for a growing software estate.

CoreLogiX is designed to support multiple software products through common operational controls. It is distinct from the customer-facing applications it may govern, and is not a substitute for their business workflows.

PLATFORM CONTEXT

A control plane with a defined internal role.

CoreLogiX is a sovereign licensing, entitlement and release-governance platform within the development estate, not a generally available customer product.
